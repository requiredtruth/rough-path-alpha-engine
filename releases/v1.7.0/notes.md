Adjustable trading panels

- Chart, Rough Path, Positions/History and Orders have minimize/restore controls. Freed space goes to the remaining panels.
- Max restores the other panels to default sizes and gives the selected panel spare space. Reset returns normal allocation.
- Layout is remembered across restart and rotation. Settings includes Reset panel sizes.
- Chart and residual canvases redraw to fill their actual allocated size. Feeds and monitoring continue while minimized.
- Small viewports scroll the workspace to keep every control reachable. No numerical indicator or research-engine changes.

- Automatic gap repair/retries pause while flat; public trades do not enable them. Amber placeholders are hidden while flat. Initial chart-history requests remain available. Native background history also skips flat market families.
- Steady host lights, fixed ordering, retained last errors including HTTP 404, on-demand event history, optional live follow and Freeze view.
