# Huge on-device risk and indicator search

- Grid, seeded random, simulated annealing with restarts, and hybrid search.
- All ten indicator dimensions plus 1–500x leverage and collateral percentage.
- Lists and min:max:step ranges, exact lazy domain counting, no duplicate evaluations, up to 1,000,000 candidates per run.
- Huge 100,000-candidate preset, full-space budget button, ETA and explicit sampled/full coverage.
- Candidate-specific risk on train, validation and untouched final holdout; saved/exported method, seed and risk settings.
- Selected indicator updates the chart; selected simulation risk is shown in the report.
- Same Android package and signing certificate for direct upgrades.

Large annual-data searches can take hours or days. Search covers configured finite steps; sampled runs do not guarantee a global optimum. Downloaded history survives cancellation; unfinished searches do not.
